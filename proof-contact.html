What Happens Next

Here's how the process works.

Fill Out the Application

Tell us about your credentials, certifications, grade levels, and current practice. The Tally form is quick and straightforward — no account needed.

We Review Your Application

Every application is personally reviewed by our team. We're looking for trained literacy specialists committed to evidence-based structured literacy instruction.

We'll Be in Touch

We respond to every application within one business day — whether that's next steps, a follow-up question, or an invitation to get onboarded.
