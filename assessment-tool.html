Assessments

Structured literacy assessments, built into the app.

The Practitioner App includes two types of assessment: a one-time initial reading assessment that establishes every new student's baseline, and ongoing Level 1–4 assessments that track progress through the structured literacy continuum.

The Level Framework

L1 Phonemic Awareness & Phonics Foundations
L2 Decoding & Word Recognition
L3 Reading Fluency & Automaticity
L4 Comprehension & Vocabulary

Results feed directly into the Lesson Builder, so every assessment turns into a plan for what to teach next.

Roster Management

Every student. Perfectly organized.

Add students to your roster, track their grade level and subject focus, and keep all their history — including every assessment result — in one place.

✓ Student profiles: name, grade, subjects, goals, and contact info
✓ Session history and practitioner notes log
✓ Active and inactive student management
✓ Parent and guardian contact records
✓ Linked directly to each student's assessment data
Available Now
Lesson Builder

Lesson plans in minutes. Not hours.

Tell the lesson builder the student's current level and learning goals — pulled straight from their assessment results. It generates a complete, structured lesson plan aligned with Orton-Gillingham principles. Edit any part of it, save it, and reuse it with future students who have the same needs.

✓ Input: student level + session goals
✓ Output: complete structured lesson plan with warm-up, main activity, and reinforcement
✓ OG methodology alignment built in
✓ Edit any section of the generated lesson
✓ Save lessons to your library for reuse
