How It Works

From sign-up to first session in minutes.

Create your tutor account

Set up your profile, enter your credentials, subjects, and availability — then choose the plan that fits your practice.

Add students and assess

Add each student to your roster, run the initial reading assessment, and let the platform build their literacy baseline automatically.

Teach, track, and grow

Use AI-generated lesson plans, log session notes, track progress through Level 1–4, and get matched with new students through K12Launch's referral network.
