How It Works

From sign-up to first session in minutes.

Create your educator account

Set up your profile, enter your credentials, subjects, and availability — then choose the plan that fits your practice.

Add students and assess

Add each student to your roster, run the initial reading assessment, and let K12Launch build their literacy baseline automatically.

Teach, track, and grow

Build lesson plans, log session notes, track progress through leveled assessments, and get matched with new students through K12Launch's referral network.
