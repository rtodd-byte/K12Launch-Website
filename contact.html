The Process

What happens after you apply.

Who We're Looking For

Built for serious literacy educators.

🔤

Credential-Verified Specialists

Orton-Gillingham trained, LETRS-certified, Wilson-certified, or equivalent structured literacy credentials. You know the methodology and you apply it.

👩‍🏫

Independent Practitioners

You work directly with students — either in-person or online. You're building a practice you're proud of and looking for better tools to support it.

📈

Ready to Grow

Whether you're just starting out or managing a full caseload, K12Launch is built to grow with your practice — from 3 students to 30.
