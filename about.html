Our Story

Built by educators. For educators.

K12Launch exists because a literacy expert and a process designer looked at how independent tutors were managing their practices — and knew there had to be a better way.

The Origin

From classroom to platform.

Katie Reel has spent her career helping children learn to read. Trained in the Orton-Gillingham approach and recognized across the South Carolina Lowcountry as a literacy expert, she has worked with hundreds of students from Pre-K through 12th grade — many of whom struggled with reading in ways that traditional classroom instruction couldn't fully address.

As she built her own tutoring practice and connected with other independent literacy educators, a pattern emerged: incredible tutors spending hours every week on admin — scheduling by text, tracking students in spreadsheets, building lesson plans from scratch, chasing invoices, and having no good way to show parents what progress actually looked like.

At the same time, Todd Reel — whose career is built on process design and operational efficiency — saw an opportunity to build something that didn't exist: a platform designed specifically for independent literacy tutors, with assessment tools grounded in structured literacy methodology, AI-powered lesson building, and a clean system for managing everything else.

K12Launch is that platform. It started with Katie's students and her methodology. It's now in beta with its first subscribers. And it's built to grow — because the more literacy educators K12Launch can support, the more children across the Lowcountry and beyond get access to the expert reading instruction they deserve.

The people behind K12Launch.

Community Partnership

Our partnership with the Lowcountry Literacy Project.

What LLP Does

The Lowcountry Literacy Project is a nonprofit dedicated to improving literacy outcomes for families across the South Carolina Lowcountry. LLP connects families to reading resources, connects educators with professional development, and advocates for literacy in the region.

Families can reach LLP through their website with questions, requests for help, or requests for resources.

What K12Launch Does

Katie Reel serves as a resource partner for LLP, reviewing incoming parent requests and routing them to the right solution:

→ Tutor requests → matched to a K12Launch tutor
→ Resource requests → curated resource library
→ Questions → K12Launch FAQ and Katie's expertise
→ Complex needs → LLP staff or other services
🤝

A formal partnership — built on a shared mission.

K12Launch and the Lowcountry Literacy Project have established a formal partnership agreement defining shared roles, referral flows, and how families are served across the region. If you're involved with LLP or interested in a partnership with K12Launch, get in touch.

Our Mission

Every child in the Lowcountry deserves expert literacy instruction.

K12Launch exists to make that possible — by giving the educators who deliver it the tools to do it well, the platform to grow their practice, and the community to support their work.
