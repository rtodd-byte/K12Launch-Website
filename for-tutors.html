Platform Features

Everything in one place.

Student Management

Your entire roster. Always organized.

Add students, track their grade level and subjects, log session notes, and review their full history — all from a single dashboard that replaces every spreadsheet you've been living in.

✓ Student profiles with grade, subject, and goal tracking
✓ Session history and notes log
✓ Active vs. inactive student status
✓ Parent/guardian contact records
Assessments

Structured literacy assessments, built in.

Start every student relationship with a comprehensive initial assessment. Then track progress through four structured literacy levels — from phonemic awareness all the way to reading comprehension.

✓ One-time initial reading assessment establishes a baseline
✓ Level 1: Phonemic Awareness & Phonics foundations
✓ Level 2: Decoding and word recognition
✓ Level 3: Reading fluency and automaticity
✓ Level 4: Comprehension and vocabulary
✓ Customizable parameters to fit your methodology
Lesson Builder

Lesson plans in minutes, not hours.

The lesson builder generates structured, OG-aligned lesson plans tailored to each student's current level and goals. Edit, save, and reuse them however you like.

✓ Generates a complete lesson from student level + goals
✓ Orton-Gillingham methodology alignment
✓ Edit any part of the generated lesson
✓ Save lessons to reuse with future students
✓ Customization modules to match your teaching style
