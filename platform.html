Assessments

Structured literacy assessments, built into the platform.

AI Lesson Builder

Lesson plans in minutes. Not hours.

Tell the AI lesson builder the student's current level and learning goals. It generates a complete, structured lesson plan aligned with Orton-Gillingham principles. Edit any part of it, save it, and reuse it with future students who have the same needs.

✓ Input: student level + session goals
✓ Output: complete structured lesson plan with warm-up, main activity, and reinforcement
✓ OG methodology alignment built in
✓ Edit any section of the generated lesson
✓ Save lessons to your library for reuse
✓ Customization tools let you shape AI output to your style

Customization Tools

The platform adapts to how you teach.

Every literacy educator has their own proven approach. K12Launch's customization modules let you modify assessment parameters and lesson templates to match your specific methodology — whether that's pure OG, Wilson, LETRS-based, or your own system.

✓ Modify assessment scoring thresholds for each level
✓ Customize lesson plan structure and section labels
✓ Create your own lesson templates to use as AI starting points
✓ Add custom skill areas to track within each level
